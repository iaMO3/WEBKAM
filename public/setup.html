WEBKAM

Connect your phone

Install WEBKAM on your iPhone or set up the certificate for your Android device.

Phone setup

Match __CERTIFICATE__ to the certificate name shown in WEBKAM on your computer.

iPhone Home Screen app Download WEBKAM profile
  1. Tap Allow to download the profile in Safari.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded, select the matching profile and tap Install. If needed, find it under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  3. Open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and enable full trust for __CERTIFICATE__.
  4. Open WEBKAM from your Home Screen, enter your computer’s pairing code, and allow camera access.
Android · Chrome Download Android certificate
  1. In Settings, search for Install a certificate and select CA certificate. The location varies by phone.
  2. Select WEBKAM.cer from Downloads.

Open the camera

Turn on full trust for __CERTIFICATE__ in Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings.
