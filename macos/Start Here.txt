WEBKAM for Mac
macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon and Intel

1. Drag WEBKAM to Applications and open it.
2. Open the VirtualCamera4Mac disk image included here. Drag VirtualCamera4Mac to Applications.
3. Open VirtualCamera4Mac from Applications, activate the extension, and approve it in System Settings when prompted.
4. Connect the phone and Mac to the same Wi-Fi network. In WEBKAM, scan the QR code with your phone and follow the pairing screen.
5. Keep WEBKAM open and select VirtualCamera4Mac as the video device in Discord, Zoom, or another calling app.

The virtual camera supports up to 1080p at 30 FPS. Each calling app chooses the output mode.

If macOS blocks WEBKAM on first launch because it is not notarized, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and use Open Anyway for WEBKAM after confirming you downloaded it from the project’s GitHub release. The bundled VirtualCamera4Mac disk image is the developer’s original download.
